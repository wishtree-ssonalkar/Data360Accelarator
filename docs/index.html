Overview

WishUnify: one view of every customer

WishUnify is Wishtree's customer data platform (CDP) accelerator. It connects a business's customer data from all its systems, works out which records belong to the same person, and turns them into a single profile per customer, ready-made analytics and plain-English answers.

The problem

Most businesses already have plenty of customer data, but it is scattered:

  • The online store knows the orders.
  • The CRM knows contact details and where the lead came from.
  • The store billing (POS) knows walk-in purchases, often only by phone number.
  • The support desk knows complaints and satisfaction scores.
  • The website / app knows browsing and abandoned carts.

The same person appears as RIYA.SHARMA@GMAIL.COM in one system, "Riya S." with phone 098765-43210 in another, and only a phone number on a store receipt. Nobody can easily answer "Who are our best customers, who is about to leave, and what should we do about it?"

Buying a commercial CDP (Salesforce Data Cloud, Adobe Real-Time CDP, Segment) solves this but is expensive, rigid and slow to customize. Building one from scratch for every client takes months.

What WishUnify does

WishUnify is a ready-made, configurable foundation that Wishtree installs for a client in days or weeks instead of months. It:

  1. Connects to the client's systems: files, cloud storage, databases and APIs.
  2. Standardizes every source into one common customer data model.
  3. Matches records across systems into one customer (by customer id, email and phone).
  4. Builds a Customer 360 profile for every person: lifetime spend, orders, favourite category, support history, website activity, segment, tier and churn risk.
  5. Provides analytics: revenue, segments, retention cohorts, conversion funnel, product performance.
  6. Answers questions in plain English through an AI assistant that writes the SQL and draws the chart.

Six capabilities

01

Connect any source

CSV, Excel, JSON, Parquet, S3, Azure Blob, Google Cloud Storage, SQL Server, Oracle, PostgreSQL, MySQL, REST APIs. Full or incremental loads.

02

Shape data around the business

Each system is mapped to a standard model of customers, orders, products, events and tickets. Mappings are built in the UI with dropdowns.

03

Customer 360 profiles

One golden record per person with RFM scores, 11 named segments, Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze tiers and churn risk.

04

Ready-made analytics

Executive overview, retention cohorts, conversion funnel, product and refund analysis, campaign audience builder.

05

Ask in plain English

"Which Gold customers in Pune haven't ordered in 90 days?" The assistant answers with a chart, data and a short summary.

06

Multi-client

Each client or business unit has separate data, sources and business rules in one installation.

What is included

  • Pipeline engine: connectors, mapping engine, identity resolution, SQL models, data-quality checks, run history.
  • Web app: 8 business pages plus 4 admin pages (clients, sources and connections, mappings, credentials and history).
  • AI assistant that works with Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Azure OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible service, chosen in the admin screens.
  • Operations: command-line tool, scheduler, Docker image, docker-compose, CI workflow, automated tests.
  • Sample data for two fictional businesses (Stride Footwear, UrbanLeaf Home) for demos.
Name and brandingThe product name, tagline and company shown in the app are set in one place (config/platform.json → branding), so the accelerator can be renamed or white-labelled for a client without code changes.
