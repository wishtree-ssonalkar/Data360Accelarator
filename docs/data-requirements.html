What data is required

WishUnify works with the data a business already has. This page explains the minimum needed to start, what unlocks each feature, every field in the standard model, accepted formats, and quality rules.

Smallest useful dataset: orders with a customer identifier One table of orders with an order id, date, value, status, channel and at least one of customer id, email or phone is enough for Customer 360, RFM segments, tiers, churn risk, retention cohorts and revenue reports.

Ready-made CSV templates

When a client cannot give direct access to a system, ask them to export into these templates. Each has the standard column names and two example rows, and a matching mapping is already included (standard_*), so no mapping work is needed: in Admin → Sources, choose "Standard template — standard_orders" (etc.) and upload the file.

customers.csv

Customer master / CRM contacts

orders.csv

Orders and receipts

order_lines.csv

Items on each order

products.csv

Product catalog

events.csv

Website / app events

tickets.csv

Support tickets

Dates in the templates use YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS; yes/no fields accept true/false, yes/no, y/n or 1/0; order status must be completed, refunded or cancelled.

Data quality rules

  • Stable ids: customer, order, line, product and ticket ids must not change between exports. Changed rows are recognised by id.
  • At least one contact key per person: email or phone (or an id shared across systems) is how records are matched. The more systems that capture email/phone, the better the 360.
  • Consistent phone country: matching uses the last N digits (10 by default for India; set per client).
  • Real dates: one format per column (the mapping converts it, e.g. dd/mm/yyyy); no future dates.
  • Clear order status: the source's values must be mappable to completed, refunded or cancelled.
  • Values without currency symbols in amount columns (e.g. 4599.00, not ₹4,599); one currency per client.
  • Product codes in order lines should match the catalog.

After each refresh WishUnify checks: orders linked to a customer (target 90%+), valid order status (100%), orders have a value, customers with an email (80%+), unique order ids, lines with a known product, tickets linked to a customer, and no future order dates. Results appear on Data pipeline → Data quality.

History and volume

  • History: at least 12 months of orders; 24+ months recommended for meaningful retention cohorts, churn and RFM.
  • Initial load: full history once; then daily or hourly updates (incremental where the source has a "modified at" column).
  • Volume: a single server handles tens of millions of rows (see sizing).

Personal data and privacy

  • WishUnify processes personal data (names, emails, phones, purchase history). The client remains the data controller; get their data-protection approval before loading.
  • Only load the fields needed. Do not load payment card data, passwords, government ids or health data; WishUnify does not need them.
  • Data stays on the server where WishUnify runs. The only external transfer is to the AI provider when the AI assistant is used (see Architecture → Security).
  • Respect marketing consent: the audience builder filters on accepts_marketing by default.
  • To delete a person, remove them from the sources (or exclude them in the mapping) and run a refresh; derived tables are rebuilt from scratch each run.
